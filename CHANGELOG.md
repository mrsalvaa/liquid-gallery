# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Pinching in Pictures scales the grid under your fingers and switches columns with an animation.
- Tabs stay alive: switch by swiping the screen or dragging along the bar.
- Requests the highest refresh rate (120/144 Hz) and the grid does less work per frame.
- Archive: removes items from Pictures but keeps them in their album and in search.
- Hidden: private vault unlocked with fingerprint, face or the phone's PIN; locks when you leave.
- Delete albums (to the trash) and hide whole albums.
- App icons: Liquid, Material, Samsung, iPhone and Motorola.

## 0.1.0

First version: pictures, viewer, videos, albums (groups, order), trash, favorites, offline search
and a basic editor, with Liquid Clock's liquid-glass interface.
