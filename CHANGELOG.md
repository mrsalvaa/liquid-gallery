# Changelog

## 0.7.1

- New photos (for example, just taken with the camera) show up when you return to the gallery, without closing and reopening it.

## 0.7.0

- Plain background: a new option in Settings, as in the other apps of the suite.
- The top and bottom selection bars now have the same liquid glass as the tab bar, also on the plain background in light theme.
- Glass and controls updated to match the rest of the suite; animations follow the system setting to remove them.

## 0.6.0

- Interface style: besides Liquid Glass (the default, with all its usual options) there is "Solid",
  with opaque cards on a plain background and no glass effects: less rounded corners, rectangular
  buttons, outline icons and a docked bottom bar. It has light or dark theme and its own accent colour.

## 0.5.0

- The app follows the phone's language: Spanish on phones set to Spanish, English everywhere else.
- Dates, months and times use the phone's language format.
- Search understands the words of the app's language ("video", "screenshot", "favorite"…).

## 0.3.2

- Fixed: swiping several times in a row towards the last tab could leave the app unresponsive.
  Tabs now use Android's standard pager.
- Fixed: closing a photo's details left the photo stuck halfway up.
- With details open, the photo shrinks to fit above the card whatever its shape (portrait,
  landscape, panorama). Close them by swiping down or tapping the photo.

## 0.2.0

- Pinching in Pictures scales the grid under your fingers and switches columns with an animation.
- Tabs stay alive: switch by swiping the screen or dragging along the bar.
- Requests the highest refresh rate (120/144 Hz) and the grid does less work per frame.
- Archive: removes items from Pictures but keeps them in their album and in search.
- Hidden: private vault unlocked with fingerprint, face or the phone's PIN; locks when you leave.
- Delete albums (to the trash) and hide whole albums.
- App icons: Liquid, Material, Samsung, iPhone and Motorola.

## 0.1.0

First version: pictures, viewer, videos, albums (groups, order), trash, favorites, offline search
and a basic editor, with Liquid Clock's liquid-glass interface.
